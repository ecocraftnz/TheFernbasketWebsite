Account

Delete your account

You can delete your The Fern Basket account, and the data linked to it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open The Fern Basket and go to Settings → Data & privacy.
  2. Tap "Delete my account and data", then confirm.

This wipes the app on that phone and deletes your account. Everything is gone from our systems within 30 days, and from backups soon after.

Without the app

Email support@thefernbasket.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days. We only act on requests sent from the account's own address, so nobody else can delete it for you.

What's deleted

  • Your account and its email address
  • Your synced recipes, basket and settings, including your name, suburb, default store and any diet setting
  • Your consent records and monthly import counts
  • Your subscriber record with RevenueCat, our payments partner

Recipes we saved from your imports can no longer be connected to you once your account is gone, and are deleted within 14 days. Copies in our providers' backups age out on their own schedule, usually within 7 to 35 days.

What we keep

A record that you asked us to delete your account, and that we did. It holds only an ID that no longer points to anyone.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in the store you bought it from:

  • Google Play: open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • App Store: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Questions? Email support@thefernbasket.com. Read our Privacy Policy.
