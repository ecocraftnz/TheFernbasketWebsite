What the app does

The app turns recipes you choose — from a link, a video, a photo or text you type — into a shopping list, and helps you shop and cook from it. Free plan and Premium

The core of the app — importing recipes within a monthly allowance, your basket, quick check, shop mode and cooking steps — is free. Premium adds features such as healthier, lower-calorie, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and dairy-free versions of recipes, and removes the monthly import limit. What's free and what's Premium is listed on the Premium screen in the app.
