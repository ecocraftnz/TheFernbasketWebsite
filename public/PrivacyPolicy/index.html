Who we are

The Fern Basket ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by Hyosun Kim, trading as "The Fern Basket", of 4B Halberg Street, Auckland 0629, New Zealand. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at support@thefernbasket.com.

We are the data controller for the personal data described below.

The short version

The app works on your device by default. There are no analytics, no ads, and we never sell or share your data for marketing. You only get an account if you choose to sign in. Three features send data off your device when you use them: importing a recipe, finding stores near you, and importing a recipe from a photo or a video. You can export or delete everything at any time.

What we deliberately don't collect

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no contacts, no device identifiers, no background or continuous location, and no IP addresses or user agents in our own consent records. We ask for exactly two OS permissions — location and photo library / camera — and only at the moment you use the feature that needs them. Declining either leaves the rest of the app fully working: you can type a suburb instead of using GPS, and import recipes by link or text instead of by photo.

Things that stay on your phone

Some data is deliberately never synced to your account, even when you're signed in:

  • Dietary and allergy tags (halal, kosher, nut-free, dairy-free and so on). These can reveal religion or a medical condition, so they stay on the device.
  • The specific store branch you picked, and your basket text size.

This is an architectural decision, not an accident. If it ever changes, this policy changes with it and we would ask for your explicit consent first.

A note on shopping lists

A basket can imply things you never typed — a religious observance, an allergy, a medical condition. We don't analyse baskets for anything beyond sorting them into aisles and estimating prices, and we don't build profiles. But it's more honest to say that a shopping list isn't neutral data than to pretend it is.

International transfers

Some of these are outside the EU/EEA, notably in the United States. Where that happens, transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or an adequacy mechanism such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the processor is certified.

OpenStreetMap is the exception worth naming. It is a volunteer project with no company to contract with, so there is no formal data processing agreement or standard transfer clause. What reaches it is only a coordinate or a place name, with nothing identifying attached.

How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud account data: while your account exists.
  • After you delete your data: erased immediately across our systems; soft-deleted rows are purged within 30 days; provider backups age out on their own schedule (roughly 7–35 days).
  • Recipe text, photos and locations sent to AI or map providers: transient. We don't store them; the provider's own API retention terms apply.
  • Consent records and deletion records: kept as proof of what was agreed and what was erased. Deletion records deliberately survive the account — they hold only an ID that no longer maps to a person.
  • Security audit log: who accessed or deleted personal data, with values hashed rather than stored. Kept for 2 years.
  • Never-verified and long-dormant accounts: the app supports automatically clearing these out, but that job is currently switched off, so no automatic deletion period applies to them yet.

Your rights

You can access, correct, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Under the NZ Privacy Act 2020 you have equivalent rights of access and correction (IPPs 6 and 7); Australian users have them under APPs 12 and 13.

In the app:

  • Access / portability: Settings → Export my data. Gives you readable JSON with two parts: what's on this device, and what's in your account — including your consent history and any deletion requests.
  • Erasure: Settings → Data & privacy → Delete my data. Wipes the device and, if you're signed in, deletes your account and your RevenueCat subscriber record.
  • Correction: edit anything directly in the app.
  • Withdraw consent: sign out to stop sync; clear your dietary default; turn off "Product news"; revoke location or photo access in your phone's settings at any time.

We'll respond to any request sent to support@thefernbasket.com within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data you can complain to your local authority: in New Zealand, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz); in Australia, the OAIC; in the EU/EEA, your national supervisory authority.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

Everything is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to cloud data is enforced per-user by database row-level security, so one account can never read another's rows. There are no passwords to leak — sign-in is a one-time code by email. Secret keys live only on our server, never in the app. Actions taken on personal data are recorded in an append-only audit log.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page and the version stamp at the top when our practices change, and surface material changes in the app. Because every consent is recorded against a policy version, we can always tell what you agreed to.
