Turn any recipe into one shopping list

The Fern Basket is a grocery app for New Zealand. Paste a recipe link, photograph a cookbook page, or share a cooking video, and it reads the ingredients into a structured list you can check and edit. Add everything you are cooking this week and the repeats collapse into single lines, so three recipes calling for butter become one entry rather than three. The finished grocery list is sorted by supermarket aisle and works on your phone without a signal, because the part of the shop that matters happens in a building with famously bad reception.

Questions people ask

Which New Zealand supermarkets does it compare?

Pak'nSave, New World and Woolworths. You can set a default store during setup and change it any time, and the app can find nearby branches using your location or a suburb you type in.

Are the supermarket prices accurate?

They are AI estimates, not live store prices, and the app says so on the screen. No New Zealand supermarket publishes a public price feed, so the comparison is there to help you choose where to shop rather than to predict your receipt. Totals are shown as approximate, the winning store is marked as likely cheapest, and what you pay at the shelf can differ.

Can I add a recipe from a YouTube video?

Yes. Send a YouTube link and the app reads the description, then any recipe page it links to, and watches the video if it has to. Links to recipe pages, plain pasted text, and photos of cookbook pages or recipe cards all work too, and a recipe in any language comes out in English. Instagram links cannot be read by apps, so for those the app asks you to copy the caption and paste it as text.

Does the shopping list work without internet?

Yes. Browsing your saved recipes, building and editing baskets, shop mode and sharing a list are all local to your phone by design. A connection is needed to add a new recipe, compare prices or find stores.

Can it adapt recipes for vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free diets?

Yes, as part of Premium. The app swaps ingredients one at a time so you can accept or skip each change, and it tells you honestly when a dish changes so much that it is really a different meal. Swapped ingredients go straight into your basket. You can also record preferences such as dairy-free, nut-free, halal, kosher, low-FODMAP, keto and others.

Is The Fern Basket free?

Yes, with a monthly allowance. A free account gets 20 recipe imports from links, photos and text, and 2 video imports, each calendar month. Every recipe you save, with no limit, your basket, quick check and shop mode, price estimates at Pak'nSave, New World and Woolworths, and sync across your devices are all free. Premium removes the import limit and adds the recipe rewriting: healthier and lower-calorie versions, and vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free versions. It starts with a seven-day free trial on a monthly or annual plan, and you can cancel any time in Settings. Prices are shown in the app.

Do I need an account?

To import a recipe, yes: a free account, because the monthly allowance is counted per account. You sign in with your email and a one-time code; there is no password. Your saved recipes, your basket and shop mode work without signing in, and signing in also syncs your recipes, baskets and settings across your devices.

What data does the app collect?

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no contacts, no device identifiers and no background location. It asks for exactly two permissions — location, and photo library or camera — and only at the moment you use the feature needing them. Dietary and allergy tags stay on your device even when you are signed in. The full privacy policy sets out the detail, and there is a page on how to delete your account.

Is it available outside New Zealand?

Not yet. The store comparison and nearby-store lookup are built around New Zealand supermarkets, so the app is New Zealand only for now.
