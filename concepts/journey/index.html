1 of 4 · Get a recipe in

Get a recipe in.

Paste a link. Share it from your browser. Or take a photo of the card in the kitchen drawer. The app keeps the ingredients and leaves the rest.

Paste an ingredient list. One ingredient per line works best.

2 of 4 · Adjust it

Adjust it.

Cooking for eight, not six? Change the serves and every amount follows. Already got something in the cupboard? Leave it out. Premium can also suggest swaps, like pumpkin for kūmara.

3 of 4 · Create the basket

One basket for the week.

Everything you’re cooking goes into one basket. Both recipes need onions, so you get one line of onions with the amounts added together. Then it’s sorted by aisle.

4 of 4 · Shop for it

Tick it off in the shop.

The basket becomes a checklist. Tick things off and they drop to the bottom, out of the way. It works without signal, which helps at the back of the chiller.

You also see an estimated total for each store. Estimates, because shelf prices change. The prices and store names in this demo are placeholders.

Premium

Do more with your recipes.

Getting recipes in, the basket and the shop are free. Premium rewrites the recipe itself, for the nights the one you’ve got isn’t quite the one you want to cook.

Improve a recipe

  • Make it healthier: smarter fats, more veg, more fibre. Same dinner.
  • Lower the calories without losing the flavour.
  • Easy swaps, one tap each. You can undo any of them.

Adapt it for who’s eating

  • Vegetarian: no meat or fish. Eggs and dairy are fine.
  • Vegan: no animal products at all.
  • Gluten-free: no wheat, barley or rye. It checks the sauces too.
  • It changes the steps as well as the ingredients, and you can make one diet your household default.

Your original recipe is always kept. Each version is saved, so you can switch back.

Where it’s at

The Fern Basket is in closed testing on Android. It works. It isn’t finished.

Works today

  • Recipes in from a link, a share from your browser, a photo of a cookbook page or card, a YouTube video, or typed out.
  • Change the serves and every amount follows.
  • One basket for everything you’re cooking, with repeats merged into one line.
  • A list sorted by aisle that you can tick off without signal.
  • Estimated basket totals, so you can see where the shop costs less.

Still rough

  • Prices are estimates. No New Zealand supermarket publishes a public price feed, so the shelf can differ.
  • Handwritten cards work when the writing is clear. Anything the app can’t read, it flags for you to check instead of guessing.
  • Android only. There’s no iPhone version yet.
  • New Zealand only for now.

Questions

Is it free? What does Premium add?

The core app is free: getting recipes in, changing the serves, the basket, the aisle-sorted list and the store estimates. Premium rewrites recipes. Improve makes one healthier or lower in calories. Adapt makes it vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free, changing the steps as well as the ingredients. Your original is always kept. Premium is monthly or annual, priced in Google Play, and starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime in Settings.

Why only Android?

It’s in closed testing on Google Play. There isn’t an iPhone version yet.

Are the prices real?

Not on this page. These are placeholders. In the app they’re estimates: no New Zealand supermarket publishes a public price feed, so what you pay can differ.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works without signing in. Sign in with your email if you want your recipes and basket on more than one device. You get a one-time code, not a password.

What does it collect?

As little as it can. No analytics and no advertising identifiers. It asks for your location, and for your camera or photos, only when you use something that needs them.
